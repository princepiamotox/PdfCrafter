DESIGNED FOR STUDENTS & TEACHERS

Not a generic PDF compressor. A lecture-to-notes workflow.

01

16:9 aware A4 layout

The app treats smart-board lecture pages as wide 16:9 slides and packs them into A4 without cropping important content.

02

Live paper cleanup

Brighten, clean backgrounds, strengthen text and sharpen diagrams while watching the page update instantly.

03

1–16 slides per sheet

Choose detailed one-slide pages or dense revision sheets for quick review and paper saving.

04

Remove unwanted pages

Select only the slides you need. Repeated, blank or irrelevant pages never have to reach the final PDF.

05

Browser-only privacy

Your PDF stays on your device. There is no account, upload queue or server-side document storage.

06

Clean professional output

A4 margins, consistent gutters, predictable placement and quality controls make the result ready for printing.
